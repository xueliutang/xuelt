Homepage

I am a Professor in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Beijing Normal University.

My research is in the analysis of partial differential equations and harmonic analysis, with a focus on the mathematical theory of fluid mechanics.

Publications and preprints·Curriculum Vitae

Research interests

  • Global well-posedness, regularity and singularity formation for nonlinear PDEs.
  • Euler and Navier–Stokes equations, the Boussinesq system, and surface quasi-geostrophic equations.
  • Vortex and temperature patches, active scalar equations, and Euler-alignment systems.
